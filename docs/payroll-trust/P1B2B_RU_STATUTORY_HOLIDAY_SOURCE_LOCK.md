# P1B2B Legal Source Lock — RU statutory public holidays / 2026

Reviewed: 2026-09-04.

## Federal non-working public holidays

Current consolidated Article 112 of the Labour Code of the Russian Federation
(ТК РФ, Federal Law No. 197-FZ, current text shown as amended 2026-05-25)
lists these federal non-working public holidays:

- January 1–6 and January 8 — New Year holidays;
- January 7 — Orthodox Christmas;
- February 23;
- March 8;
- May 1;
- May 9;
- June 12;
- November 4.

Consolidated source:
https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_34683/98ef2900507766e70ff29c0b9d8e2353ea80a1cf/

Official legal information portal:
https://pravo.gov.ru/

## Regional boundary

Subjects of the Russian Federation may establish additional non-working
public holidays. Therefore a federal-negative answer is not a complete
statutory-negative answer for an arbitrary region.

Reference:
https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_311098/

## 2026 transferred rest days

Government Resolution of the Russian Federation No. 1466 of 2025-09-24
transfers rest days in 2026. The act is officially published here:

https://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202509240023

The transfer mechanism does not create a new Article-112 federal statutory
holiday identity. P1B2B therefore keeps transferred rest-day authority separate
from federal public-holiday authority.

## Trust boundary

Legal regime ID used by code:
`RU_TK_RF_ARTICLE_112_CALENDAR_2026_V1`

Supported calendar window:
`2026-01-01 <= date < 2027-01-01`

Outside that window P1B2B fails closed. This is intentionally conservative:
the stage claims only the legal calendar year that has been explicitly
source-locked here.

Regional holiday policy is not implemented by this stage.
